CreditShield Soft Review Rules (SR-01 — SR-05)

SR-01 — Income coefficient of variation
- Condition: Income CV > 30% on verified inflows (AA / bank analytics).
- Action: MANUAL REVIEW.
- Reference: Variable income playbook.
- Exceptions: Seasonal occupation codes with documented seasonality factor may downgrade severity (analyst decision).
- Example: Freelancer CV 34% → SR-01.

SR-02 — Missing ITR and GST (self-employed)
- Condition: Employment class = self-employed AND no ITR AND no GST where GST registration is expected for stated turnover.
- Action: MANUAL REVIEW.
- Reference: Self-employed income verification SOP.
- Exceptions: Profession codes exempt from GST by statute (documented).
- Example: Consultant, no ITR, no GST → SR-02.

SR-03 — Fraud probability band
- Condition: Model fraud probability between 25% and 50%.
- Action: MANUAL REVIEW.
- Reference: Fraud analytics policy.
- Exceptions: None auto above 50% (escalate to SR-03 high band / HR as applicable).
- Example: Fraud score 32% → SR-03.

SR-04 — Salary gap
- Condition: Gap between salary credits >2 months in lookback window.
- Action: MANUAL REVIEW.
- Reference: Payroll continuity checks.
- Exceptions: Documented leave / maternity with employer letter.
- Example: No salary credit for 3 months → SR-04.

SR-05 — New-to-credit
- Condition: Bureau age <12 months.
- Action: MANUAL REVIEW.
- Reference: Thin-file programme rules.
- Exceptions: Secured gold / LAP with strong collateral may use alternate scorecard (manual).
- Example: First bureau trade 8 months old → SR-05.
