CreditShield Loan Programmes — Eligibility snapshot (illustrative; board-approved tables are source of truth)

Personal Loan
- Typical verified income minimum: ₹25,000/month (metro tier may differ).
- FOIR cap: 65% (auto engine).
- Typical CIBIL floor: 550 unsecured.
- Tenure: 12–60 months (programme dependent).

Home Loan
- Typical verified income minimum: ₹35,000/month.
- FOIR cap: 65% (some LTV bands stricter).
- Typical CIBIL floor: 600.
- Tenure: up to 30 years subject to retirement age policy.

MSME / Business
- Typical minimum annual turnover evidence: programme-specific; often GST + 12M banking.
- FOIR / DSCR blended metrics apply; FOIR auto rule may be supplemented by analyst DSCR.
- Tenure: 12–84 months common for working capital term loans.

Vehicle Loan
- Typical verified income minimum: ₹20,000/month.
- FOIR cap: 65% with LTV adjustments.
- Secured bureau floors typically lower than unsecured personal but policy still enforces HR-06 variant by product code.

BNPL
- Typical verified income minimum: ₹15,000/month for revolving BNPL lines.
- Lower ticket sizes; stricter velocity and stacking checks (HR-05 especially).
- Tenure: revolving; pay-in-4 products use shorter windows.

Risk score bands (engine output 0–100)
- 0–35 REJECT
- 36–50 REVIEW
- 51–65 CAUTION
- 66–80 APPROVE
- 81–100 PREMIUM APPROVE
